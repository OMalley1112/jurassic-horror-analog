# ANA LOG — HORROR JURASSIC PARK

Analog-horror survival-horror prototype. Everything (map, props, player model,
sounds, VHS effect) is generated by code — no external art files.

## BUILD THE MAP (2 steps)

1. Open this project in **Unity 2022.3** and let it finish importing/compiling.
2. Menu bar → **Jurassic Horror → Build Complete Map && Player**
   - creates `Assets/Scenes/JurassicHorror.unity` (saved + added to Build Settings)
   - press **Play**

The builder is deterministic (seed 1993): re-running it always produces the
same map.

## MAIN MENU

Pressing Play opens a **title screen** (`MainMenu`): a dark plate with
scanlines over the live island feed, a chromatically-bled `JURASSIC PARK`
title and the tape label `▌ ISLAND SECURITY TAPE 04 — RECOVERED FOOTAGE`.

- **BEGIN TAPE** / **SETTINGS** / **CONTROLS** / **QUIT** — navigate with W/S
  or the mouse, confirm with Enter/Space/E/click. The CONTROLS card lists
  every key.
- **SETTINGS** — a live-adjustable options page: **mouse sensitivity**,
  **field of view**, **master volume**, **invert Y axis** and **head bob**,
  each as a bar you can click/drag or nudge with A/D, plus **RESET DEFAULTS**.
  Values apply instantly and persist in `PlayerPrefs` (see `GameSettings`).
- The OS arrow is hidden everywhere the menu or pause card is open — you get
  the game's own **drawn tape-reticle cursor** instead: a jittering crosshair
  with corner brackets, chromatic red/cyan ghosts, a slow wobble and an
  expanding capture-frame ripple on every click (`DrawnCursor`).
- While the menu is open the whole game is gated: player frozen, no HUD,
  no battery drain, no hunts — but the island keeps breathing behind
  the glass (lightning, static bursts, slow camera drift).
- **BEGIN TAPE** unlocks everything, resets the run clock, locks the cursor
  and glitches the tape as it "starts rolling". Restarting after an ending
  skips the menu (one menu per play session).

## PLAYER BODY & ANIMATION

The first-person body (`PlayerModelBuilder`, primitives only) is a ranger
with a field jacket (straps, chest pouches, radio + antenna), knee/elbow
pads, knee-high boots with soles, a face (brow, nose, ears) under the ranger
hat, a backpack with bedroll straps and the held flashlight.

`PlayerBodyAnimator` drives a real 4-part rig — hips, torso, thigh→**knee
pivot**→shin/boot, shoulder→**upper arm**→**elbow pivot**→hand — so the body
follows movement:

- **Run**: stride grows 42°→58°, arms pump harder (0.7→1.05), elbows stay
  bent around the light, torso leans up to 15°, hips bob with each stride.
- **Crouch** (`stanceT` fed live from the controller): hips drop to 0.55 m
  and shift back, thighs fold forward 70°, knees bend 76°, torso leans 26°
  and sinks, arms tuck in.
- **Airborne**: legs tuck up (thigh −24°, knee +42°) and the torso pitches.
- **Idle**: breathing sway on torso + hips.

## THE GAME (objective)

**Recover 3 power cells and escape through the main gate.**

- The gate arch at z=22 is dead — walk up to it first and the tape tells you
  why. A persistent objective line + cell pips live in the HUD (top-left).
- **Power cells** (glowing, bobbing, green point light — one per danger zone):
  1. **Visitor lobby** — on the floor by the reception desk
  2. **Tunnel end** — in front of the red bunker door, 40 m underground
  3. **T-Rex paddock** — beside the crushed jeep… the rex is standing over it
- **Taking a cell screams over the grid**: every raptor within 55 m gets your
  exact position (`ObjectiveSystem.Collect → ReceiveAlert`). Decide before
  you grab it: lamp off, crouched, escape route memorized.
- With all 3 cells, reach the gate → the **T-Rex showdown**
  (`BossFight`). A letterboxed intro film (2 dolly shots, captions
  `EXT. MAIN GATE — THE ROAD OUT IS TAKEN` / `IT IS NOT LETTING YOU LEAVE`,
  **R** skips) drops it onto the road out — then it's a real fight:
  **LMB fires the flare gun** (0.75 s recharge, ~14° aim cone, 34 m range)
  and **4 stagger hits** drive it back into the jungle. The HUD shows a
  4-segment stagger bar and flinch state; every hit is a glitch + shake.
  Die mid-fight and the next approach starts straight in the fight — the
  film only ever plays once per run. Then the road is clear → the
  **ending cutscene** plays
  (`EndingCutscene`): letterboxed film shots of the walk-out with scene
  captions (`EXT. MAIN GATE — NIGHT`), a crane up over the island with
  photo-flash lightning, then a **credits roll** — after which the
  `END OF TAPE` card lands with survival time, cells and tape cuts.
  **R** skips the film to the card; press **R** again to rewind to the start.

### Survival mechanics
- **Stamina** — sprinting drains the bar (bottom-left); when it cracks you're
  forced to walk until you recover past the floor. Sprint is not a right.
- **Crouch stealth** — hal detection range, half footstep volume. T-Rex and
  raptors genuinely lose you in the ferns if the lamp is off.
- **Your flashlight is a beacon** — it *increases* detection range by 35 %,
  and if you sweep the beam across a dinosaur it spots you instantly from up
  to 46 m, even if you're crouched in cover.
- **Pack AI** — when one raptor starts a chase, the others are told your last
  position and swing out to *flank* you (they encircle, not beeline),
  answering with short barks from the dark.
- **Tape cuts** — the death counter follows you to the end card.
- **Dying is a jumpscare** — when a dinosaur reaches you, a procedurally
  built head (rex or raptor) **lunges at the lens**, jaws snap shut on a
  white flash with a full-volume roar and screen shake (`JumpScare`), the
  tape is torn to static, and you respawn at spawn with every hunter reset.

### Talking survivors (`NpcSurvivor`)
Three stranded survivors you can walk up to and **press E** to talk to:
- **MAYA** (visitor services) — east lobby of the Visitor Center
- **RANGER BOONE** — Ranger Station 07, across the river
- **VASQUEZ** — geothermal plant canopy

Dialogue locks you in place (talking in the dark is a commitment) while the
NPC turns to face you — body *and* head track you — and speaks in short
procedural voice blips. A bottom subtitle box shows their name (amber) and
line; `E  CONTINUE` advances, and each one finishes with a lore/hint line
about the cells, the rex, or the gate. One conversation at a time island-wide.

### Analog horror
- VHS post shader: scanlines, chromatic aberration, tape wobble, tracking
  tears, static bursts + REC/timecode viewfinder HUD.
- 6 **CreepyFigure** silhouettes with glowing eyes (4 original + one on the
  cargo container, one in the aviary). They only move when you
  are *not* looking — slowly turning to face you and leaning in. Look back and
  it freezes mid-lean; get close while it's in view and it vanishes with a
  static burst + sting sound.
- Fully procedural audio: looping dread drone, random distant roars, footsteps,
  flashlight clicks, scare stings (no audio files).

### The horror layer (`Assets/Scripts/Horror/`)
- **They hunt you.** The T-Rex and all three raptors run `DinoHunter`: they
  notice you by line-of-sight, by your flashlight sweeping them, or by you
  *staring at them too long* — then they close in with heavy, ground-shaking
  footsteps. You run at 5.4, the rex at 4.5 and raptors at 5.0, so escape is
  possible but never comfortable.
- **Caught = tape eaten.** If one reaches you, full static, a `◀◀ REWIND`
  frame, camera slam, then the tape "respoools" you back at the spawn point
  while every dinosaur resets to its post. Cooldown prevents instant repeats.
- **Tension system** — a heartbeat that accelerates as danger closes, driving
  camera micro-shake and constant low-level tracking wobble.
- **Storm** — irregular multi-strobe lightning that bleaches the island white,
  thunder delayed by distance, practical lights surging, occasional
  `⚠ EMERGENCY BROADCAST` tape messages.
- **Dread events** — whispers right behind your shoulder, phantom footsteps
  that walk toward you and stop, sudden flashlight blackouts that stutter
  before relighting, distant fence impacts, a **shadow that crosses the road
  22–36 m ahead** (then a static burst), and a **dead radio** that blips a
  garbled voice from somewhere in the dark with a `RADIO ▌ …` subtitle.
- **The idleness hunter** — stand still for 14 s under tension and the tape
  starts hunting *you*: whispers/footsteps plus
  `⚠ SUBJECT STATIONARY — MOVEMENT ADVISED`. Keep moving.

### Set dressing added by the horror pass
- **5 rotating red emergency beacons** (watchtower sweep, gate-arch spin,
  visitor-roof strobe, geothermal-plant strobe, aviary strobe).
- **Moonlight shafts** — translucent volumes hanging under the two roof holes,
  with a drifting **dust-mote** particle field.
- **Cave reverb** over the whole underground tunnel zone: your footsteps
  suddenly sound like they're happening to someone else.

### The animation pass (`MapBuilder.Animation.cs` + `DinoAnimator`)
- **Dinosaurs walk for real.** The T-Rex and raptors are rigged with joint
  pivots built by the builder (torso → hip → knee → ankle, chained tail
  segments, hinged jaw) and driven by `DinoAnimator`:
  - distance-synced **walk/sprint cycle** — legs alternate, knees fold on the
    trailing step, ankles counter-rotate so feet stay planted, body bobs and
    rolls with the stride
  - breathing torso + slow head scans when idle, travelling **tail wave**
    that runs from hips to tip (much wider while running)
  - occasional jaw flicks, and a full **gape + head-down lunge** the instant
    it catches you
  - the pteranodon gets nervous wing-fold twitches (`DinoIdle.wingL/R`)
- **Swinging props** (`Swing` — two overlaid sine waves, random phase):
  the lobby **WAY OUT sign on its chains**, the **PARK CLOSED sign** (real
  chains added, swaying over the gate arch), the **raptor gate** creaking on
  its hinge, and all **12 roof vines** twitching around the roof holes.
- **The moon shaft breathes** (`LightPulse`): slow intensity drift plus a
  faint electrical flutter so the light over the skeleton never sits still.
- **Catch fixed** — `DinoHunter` now actually calls
  `HorrorDirector.CatchPlayer()` at `catchRange` (the rewind used to be
  unreachable), and exposes `CurrentSpeed` so the walk cycle matches the
  exact ground speed, wind-up included.

### The survival pass (`MapBuilder.Objective.cs` + game loop)
- **Real game loop**: `ObjectiveSystem` (objective, pickups, gate, escape,
  restart) + `GameHud` (objective line, cell pips, stamina bar, prompt,
  end card) + `FuseCell` (bobbing glowing pickups with a pickup click/flare).
- **Player**: stamina-governed sprint, crouch (camera drops to 58 % height,
  quieter steps), exposed as `IsCrouched` / `IsSprinting` / `Stamina01`.
- **AI**: stance- and light-sensitive detection (`EffectiveDetectRange`),
  real flashlight-sweep provocation (`BeamReveals`), raptor pack alerts with
  flanking (`DinoHunter.Pack`, `ReceiveAlert`, `InvestigateStep`), `Freeze()`
  for the ending.
- **Audio**: seamless `WindLoop` jungle-wind bed added to the drone.
- **HorrorDirector.CatchCount** tracks deaths for the end card.

### The Director's Cut pass
- **UI typography overhaul** — every on-screen text is now small, muted and
  game-like: 10–13 px OS fonts (`HudFonts` — Segoe UI / Consolas, no font
  assets), alerts render as quiet **bottom subtitles** instead of giant
  centered marquees, the end/pause cards use restrained 30/28 px titles, and
  a 2 px **center dot** replaces nothing louder. Sprint adds a **FOV kick**
  (`runFovBoost`, crouch tightens it).
- **Bone bait (Q)** — 5 bones per run. Throw one and every hunter within
  45 m (`BaitPellet` → `ReceiveAlert`) goes to investigate the clatter —
  **including the T-Rex**. It's the tool that makes the paddock cell fair:
  toss a bone to the far side of the jeep, steal the cell while the rex is
  busy. Chasing predators stay committed (baits never interrupt a hunt).
- **Escalation** — every power cell makes the island angrier
  (`ObjectiveSystem`): **1** → storm gaps shrink to 62 %, **2** → the
  **T-Rex breaks out** of its paddock and re-anchors on the west road
  branch between the breach and the gate (`DinoHunter.Redeploy`, with a
  60 s pathing failsafe), **3** → raptors gain +0.35 speed and +15 %
  detection ("all signatures mobile").
- **Death cam** — the tape now holds half a second on the thing that got
  you (`FirstPersonController.SnapLookAt` + `hunter.eyeHeight`) before the
  static eats the frame.
- **Threat vignette** — the view edges bleed dark red as `displayedTension`
  rises (radial texture generated at runtime in `GameHud`).
- **Tape pause (Esc / click)** — `PauseSystem`: `timeScale 0`,
  `AudioListener.pause`, pause card. Esc/click resumes.

### The showdown pass (`BossFight`) — bigger map, second film, bug fixes
- **Boss fight at the gate** (`Assets/Scripts/Horror/BossFight.cs`, wired
  lazily by `ObjectiveSystem.UpdateGate` once all 3 cells are in):
  - **Intro film** — 2 letterboxed dolly shots (`BossFight.CutsceneRunning`
    makes the gameplay HUDs stand down), a lunge + roar + shake on shot 2,
    pause-aware **R SKIP**. The block position is picked around the
    player/gate on the plaza (≥5.5 m from you, clamped to x −9..9 z 10..20.5).
  - **The fight** — infinite flares on LMB (muzzle flash light, procedural
    `NoiseBurst` report, recoil shake), `detect/lose/beamRange = 200` and a
    `ForceHunt()` re-commit every frame so the blockade never breaks chase;
    4 hits (`Dot ≥ 0.97` aim cone within 34 m) → freeze + recoil coroutine
    + glitch + broadcast per stagger, `hits/4` segments over the crosshair.
  - **Win** — `WinRoutine` zeroes its senses and calls
    `DinoHunter.Retreat()`; it walks off the set toward the west road
    (normal return-home gait, 2.5 s) → `Won = true` → the ending film rolls.
  - **Lose** — the usual jumpscare; `introShown` persists so the retry
    starts straight in the fight (the film is never replayed).
- **Ranch 04** — the island grew again: a whole far-north breeding-ranch
  district (see *The expansion* above), appended after the JP III set
  pieces so it never shifts the verified map above it.
- **Bug fixes this pass** (offline compile EXIT 0, scene rebuilt,
  `scene_audit` FAILURES: 0):
  1. **Pause can no longer unfreeze held states** — the pause menu's freeze
     is a separate flag (`SetPauseFrozen`) from the external hold, so
     unpausing during a cutscene / catch / NPC dialogue no longer hands the
     player back mid-sequence (`FirstPersonController`, `PauseSystem`).
  2. **NPC dialogue ignores keys while paused** (`NpcSurvivor`).
  3. **Double-tap R can't skip the end card into an accidental restart** —
     0.8 s grace after the card lands (`ObjectiveSystem`).
  4. **Dread events stop at the ending** — no whispers/blackouts after the
     escape, and a blackout in progress can't relight the lamp over the
     ending film (`DreadEvents`).
  5. **HUDs stand down when they should** — flashlight status never draws
     over the title screen, boss intro film or ending (`Flashlight`,
     `GameHud`); lamp keys are dead after the escape.
  6. **Ending film R-skip is pause-aware** in both the shot loop and the
     credits (`EndingCutscene`).
  7. **Null-AudioClip warnings fixed at the root** — roaring/screeching
     dinosaurs spammed `PlayOneShot was called with a null AudioClip`
     after a script recompile during play (the domain reload drops
     references to runtime-made AudioClips). All baked dino clips and the
     player's footstep clips are now **self-healing**: they re-bake on the
     spot instead of going silent (`DinoHunter.Pick`,
     `FirstPersonController.BakeFootsteps`).

## TIPS

- Tweak fog/lighting: select nothing → `Lighting` settings, or edit
  `MapBuilder.cs → BuildLighting()`.
- Re-run **Jurassic Horror → Build Complete Map && Player** any time to
  rebuild from scratch (it creates a fresh empty scene first).
- Materials live in `Assets/Resources/Materials/` — edit colors there.
- Scripts: `Assets/Scripts/Player` (controller, body model, flashlight),
  `Assets/Scripts/AnalogHorror` (VHS, audio, materials),
  `Assets/Scripts/Horror` (director, tension, storm, dread, dinosaur AI +
  `DinoAnimator`, gate showdown `BossFight`), `Assets/Scripts/World` (flicker, figures, beacons, reverb,
  `Swing`, `LightPulse`), `Assets/Scripts/Audio` (procedural soundscape).
- Builder code: `Assets/Editor/MapBuilder*.cs`.
